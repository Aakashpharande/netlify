Akash Pharande is the Managing Director of Pharande Spaces — one of Maharashtra's most trusted real estate developers. With a unique blend of technical expertise, business acumen, and strategic foresight, he has been a pivotal force in shaping integrated township development across Pune.

As a hands-on leader, he actively oversees every stage of project development: from land acquisition and regulatory approvals through infrastructure planning, execution, and delivery — ensuring every project reflects the highest standards of quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.
